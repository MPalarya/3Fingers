Install in about a minute.

3Fingers is free and not sold through the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm a couple of things the first time.

  1. Download and drag to Applications

    Download 3Fingers.dmg, open it, and drag the 3Fingers icon onto the Applications folder.

  2. Open it for the first time

    Open 3Fingers from your Applications folder. macOS will warn you that it can't verify the developer. This is normal for free apps distributed outside the App Store.

    macOS 15 Sequoia and newer: click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

    macOS 14 Sonoma: right-click (or Control-click) 3Fingers in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again.

  3. Allow Accessibility access

    macOS will ask to let 3Fingers control your computer. Click Open System Settings and switch on 3Fingers under Accessibility.

    This is the permission every mouse and trackpad utility needs to send clicks. 3Fingers doesn't read what you type or what's on your screen.

  4. Tap with three fingers

    Look for the hand icon in your menu bar near the clock. Tap a link with three fingers and it opens in a new tab. Click the icon to turn 3Fingers off or start it at login.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. 3Fingers is free and open source under the MIT license. There are no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

Is it safe?

The full source code is public on GitHub, and every download is built automatically from that code. The app never connects to the internet and collects no data.

macOS shows a warning only because the app isn't registered with Apple's paid developer program.

Three-finger tap already does something on my Mac.

If three-finger tap is set to look up words, change it so both actions don't fire at once. Go to System Settings → Trackpad → Point & Click, and under Look up & data detectors choose Force Click with One Finger.

Does it work with an Intel Mac or an older macOS?

No. 3Fingers is built for Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4 and newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

It stopped working after an update.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, remove 3Fingers with the – button, then open the app again and turn it back on when asked.

How do I uninstall it?

Click the hand icon in the menu bar, choose Quit 3Fingers, then drag 3Fingers from Applications to the Trash.

I'm a developer. Can I install it from Terminal?

With Homebrew:

brew tap mpalarya/3fingers https://github.com/MPalarya/3Fingers brew trust mpalarya/3fingers brew install --cask 3fingers

Or with curl:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MPalarya/3Fingers/main/install.sh | bash

Both methods skip the "unverified developer" warning. See the README for details and build instructions.
